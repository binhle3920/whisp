An AI layer between your inbox and you.

Whisp watches your Gmail, asks an AI assistant what each new email means for you, and sends a short, personal briefing to Telegram. Marketing mail waits for one evening digest instead of interrupting your day.

How it works

1 · Read

Gmail

New messages are picked up with read-only access. Whisp never changes your mailbox.

2 · Understand

Your AI assistant

A model of your choice on OpenRouter writes a briefing tuned to your role and priorities.

3 · Notify

Telegram

The briefing lands in your chat. Promotions are batched into a daily digest.

Talk to your inbox

Reply to a notification or message the bot directly. The assistant can search your mail, open an email, read PDF and document attachments, and follow links you or your email mention — then answer in the chat.

Built to be trusted

  • Self-hosted. Runs on your laptop, home server, or VPS with a single SQLite file.
  • Read-only Gmail. It requests the narrowest scope it needs.
  • Email is untrusted input. Content is fenced off from instructions to resist prompt injection.
  • No secrets in logs. Tokens and request URLs are kept out of error output.
  • Open source. Licensed under AGPL-3.0-or-later.
